The Concept

A living room. Low lights.
Music that finds you up close.

L'Intime gathers a handful of guests in my living room for an evening of live, unplugged sessions. No stage, no crowd — just armchairs, candlelight, a glass in hand, and the artist breathing the same air as the audience.

Between songs, people talk. Before the music, they meet. After it, they linger. That's the whole idea.
